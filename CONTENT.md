### Tire Type Descriptions (for filter labels + detail pages)

**All-Season**
The most popular choice. Designed for year-round performance in dry, wet, and light snow conditions. Fitted on most new cars from the factory. Long tread life and quiet ride.
*Best for: Daily drivers, sedans, minivans, crossovers*

**Summer / Performance**
Maximum grip in warm conditions. Stickier rubber compound delivers sharper handling and shorter braking distances. Must be swapped before temperatures drop below 45°F.
*Best for: Sports cars, performance sedans, enthusiast builds*

**Winter / Snow**
Softer rubber stays flexible in cold temperatures for superior traction on ice and packed snow. Deep sipes channel slush away from the contact patch. Three-Peak Mountain Snowflake rated.
*Best for: Colorado winters, mountain driving, trucks and SUVs*

**All-Terrain (A/T)**
Bigger tread blocks than all-season for confident off-road capability while remaining daily-driveable. Good on dirt, gravel, and light snow. Moderate road noise.
*Best for: Trucks, SUVs, Jeeps, light off-road use*

**Off-Road / Mud-Terrain (M/T)**
Maximum traction in mud, rocks, and technical terrain. Aggressive void pattern self-cleans. Louder on highway. Built to handle abuse.
*Best for: Rock crawlers, serious off-roaders, overlanding rigs*

**Performance / Low-Profile**
Ultra-low sidewall for precision handling and aggressive stance. Wide contact patch maximizes grip. Stiff ride — designed for sport and modified vehicles.
*Best for: Modified cars, stance builds, track-day vehicles*

### Wheel Finish Descriptions

**Chrome** — High-gloss mirror finish. The brightest, showiest look available. Classic and attention-getting.

**Black Machined** — Gloss or satin black with machined face accents for contrast. Modern and aggressive.

**Matte Black** — Flat, non-reflective black finish. Clean, stealth look that pairs with any color.

**Alloy Silver** — Brushed or polished silver aluminum. Clean OEM-upgrade look. Understated.

**Bronze / Gunmetal** — Warm earth-tone finish. Popular with truck and Jeep builds. Works with both lifted and lowered setups.
