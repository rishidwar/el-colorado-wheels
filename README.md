## Key Selling Points (from 484 reviews)

1. **Affordable** — mentioned in 37 reviews, #1 brand attribute
2. **Flat tire repair** — 32 reviews, most common service
3. **Used tires available** — 17 reviews
4. **No appointment needed** — 6 reviews, big convenience win
5. **Fast / no wait** — in-and-out in under 20 mins (per reviews)
6. **Honest** — never pushed to buy extra (Hector specifically praised)
7. **Winter tires + seasonal swap** — seasonal service demand
